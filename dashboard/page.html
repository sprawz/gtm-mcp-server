GOOGLE SIGN-IN

Add the server URL to your MCP client, then choose its sign-in option. Complete Google consent in the browser.

Setup needed

The operator must configure GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET, register the Google callback URL, and restart the server.
